Features

The autonomous engine built for maximum retention.

Every capability engineered to eliminate manual scrubbing, editing, and publishing.

Dual-Judge AI Virality Engine

A single AI model misses the context. ClipCut pairs a semantic Words Judge (analyzing humor, hooks, and punchlines) with an acoustic Reaction Judge (monitoring audio dB spikes and Twitch/YouTube live chat velocity). Clips are created only when both models agree.

Active Speaker Face Tracking

No awkward static crops. Computer vision automatically tracks the speaker's face across wide-angle VODs and multi-person podcast cameras, dynamically centering 9:16 framing without jarring jumps.

Kinetic Captions & 3D Emojis

ClipCut runs silently in the background. It polls Twitch, Kick, and YouTube channels, detects live streams within 15 seconds, and begins generating clips while the broadcast is still live.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Everything you need to know about ClipCut's autonomous clipper, models, and licensing.

Is ClipCut a monthly subscription?

No. You pay once ($29) and receive 12 full months of access with $29 of AI credit included. There are no surprise monthly credit card renewals, and you can top up more credits only if you need them.

How much video can I clip with $29?

A tremendous amount. $29 covers many dozens of hours of live stream monitoring and video VODs. Rendering, face tracking, and 3D caption styling are entirely free of surcharge.

How does the 24/7 stream watcher work?

You add a Twitch, Kick, or YouTube streamer's URL once. ClipCut checks the channel's status every minute. When live, it attaches to the stream buffer, transcribes audio chunks, runs retention scoring, and outputs ready-to-publish vertical clips in real time.

What caption styles and languages are supported?

ClipCut includes 3 primary styling engines: Bounce (energetic scale transitions with 3D emojis), Hype (cyber neon glow with color-coded hooks), and Clean (minimalist subtitles). It supports English, phonetic Hinglish romanization, and multilingual speech.

Can I self-host ClipCut?

Yes! ClipCut is open source on GitHub (https://github.com/CM-Studio77/Clipcut). You can clone the repo and run it locally with Docker and your own Whisper or Gemini API keys.
