Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions about ClipCut? Here are the straightforward answers.

Does ClipCut work with Hindi and Hinglish videos?
Yes! ClipCut integrates multilingual Whisper ASR and a smart transliteration system. It transcribes Hindi audio and spells the subtitles in clean, natural Roman letters (Hinglish like "bhai ye kya hua") with per-word bounce and highlight animations.
How does the Facecam Split layout work?
ClipCut uses visual AI to locate the streamer's webcam in the 16:9 video frame. With Facecam TOP 🔼, the webcam is cropped and pinned to the top 800px, while gameplay fills the bottom 1120px. Facecam BOTTOM 🔽 places gameplay on top and webcam on bottom.
Is ClipCut really free and open source?
Yes! The entire project is open source on GitHub. You can host it on your own computer or VPS, plug in any free AI API key (like Google Gemini, OpenAI, or local models), and make unlimited clips without watermarks.
What video sources can I use?
You can paste any YouTube video, VOD or Shorts link, Twitch streams & clips, Kick live channels, or directly drag-and-drop your local MP4, MKV, MOV, or WebM video files.
