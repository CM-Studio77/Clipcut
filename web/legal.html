What we collect

  • Account. Email address, name, and either a password (stored only as a one-way scrypt hash) or, with Sign in with Google, your Google account ID, name and email. Whether you have confirmed your email.
  • What you give us to clip. Links you paste, files you upload, creators and channel links you add, and your settings.
  • What we make from it. Downloaded videos, transcripts, loudness and chat-activity figures, clips, titles, captions and scores.
  • Public channel details. For channel links you add: the channel's public name, picture, follower count and live status from YouTube, Twitch or Kick.
  • Connected accounts. Access tokens from YouTube or Instagram if you connect them to post clips.
  • Credits and payments. Balance, purchases (amount, date, the payment provider's reference), expiries and a record of each AI check. We never receive or store card details.
  • Technical data. Session records with IP address and browser, and server logs, used for security and to keep the Service running.

Why we use it

  • To provide the Service you asked for: downloading, transcribing, judging, cutting, captioning and posting clips (performance of our contract with you).

  • To send account emails such as email confirmation and password resets (contract and legitimate interests).
  • To keep accounting and tax records (legal obligation).

We do not sell your information, do not use it for advertising, and do not use your videos or clips to train AI models.

Who else handles it

  • Google (AI services) receives transcript text and loudness and chat figures for the moments being judged, to choose clips and write titles and captions. Speech is transcribed on our own servers.
  • Our payment provider processes payments and tells us when one succeeds.
  • Our email provider delivers account emails.
  • Our hosting providers store and run the Service.
  • YouTube, Instagram, Twitch and Kick receive the requests needed to fetch content and public channel details and, when you choose, the clips you post.

Some of these providers process data outside your country. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.
